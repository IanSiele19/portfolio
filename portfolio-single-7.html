Rehab Connect is a mental health support concept designed to make it easier for families to find rehabilitation centres and connect with psychiatrists when help is needed.

The idea behind the project is to reduce the stress families face when searching for trusted care by organizing contact details, treatment options, and professional support in one clearer experience. I focused on building a user-first interface that feels compassionate, informative, and easy to navigate.
