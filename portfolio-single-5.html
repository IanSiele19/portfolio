Sanctuary Spa is a luxury wellness website designed to highlight spa treatments, wellness services, and a relaxing brand identity.

This project focused on creating a clean and elegant user experience with responsive sections for services, pricing, and contact information. I designed and structured the site to feel calm, premium, and easy to navigate on both desktop and mobile devices.
